# Product brief

## Description

The Big Blue PCM Recovery & Tuning Program documents a gated recovery and tuning process for a 2000 Chevrolet Silverado 2500 with a P01 PCM, 6.0L LQ4 engine, and 4L80E transmission.

## Primary audience

The primary reader is the team recovering and validating the Big Blue truck. This is inferred from the project and issue descriptions.

## Jobs to be done

- Recover and preserve the truck's original PCM image.
- Validate the reported original OS before changing calibrations.
- Identify stock calibrations and matching definitions that fit the engine, transmission, hardware, driveline, and emissions configuration.
- Prepare a controlled flash only after every compatibility gate passes.

## Motivation

The program separates research from flash approval. It reduces the risk of using a plausible but incompatible calibration or definition on hardware that must remain recoverable.
