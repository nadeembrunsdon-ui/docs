This inventory evaluates stock calibration evidence for the Big Blue P01 PCM, 6.0L LQ4, and 4L80E combination. It separates an available file from a calibration that is safe to flash.

## Decision summary

Do not approve a 2002 OS update yet. The often-proposed `12212156` is listed as a 2002 LS1 OS, while `12209203` is listed as the 2002 truck OS. No sourced `12209203` BIN in this review proves the complete 6.0L LQ4 and 4L80E combination.

The strongest reference found is a stock `12202088` image from a 2001 GMC Sierra 2500 with a 6.0L LQ4, 4L80E, and 4x2 driveline. It has an OS-matched XDF, but its emissions configuration is unknown. Treat it as a comparison candidate, not a flash-approved image.

## Evidence standard

This page uses three states:

- **Matched** means the BIN identifies the expected OS and a separately sourced XDF names the same OS.
- **Partial** means at least one required compatibility attribute is missing or comes only from a community filename.
- **Not approved** means the evidence does not support flashing the image to Big Blue.

An OS-matched XDF is not proof that every table or parameter is correct. The XDF repository states that most definitions have not been tested by its maintainer. Review changes at the byte level and verify checksums with a second tool before any write.

## Compatibility gates

Complete every gate before promoting a candidate from research to flash planning.

1. **Recover the original.** Save at least two identical full reads of Big Blue's PCM. Record the PCM service number, hardware ID, reported OS ID, segment IDs, VIN, and cryptographic hash.
2. **Confirm the target configuration.** Record 2WD or 4WD, axle ratio, tire size, electronic throttle or cable throttle, and every transmission-related option.
3. **Confirm emissions content.** Match the under-hood emissions label and RPO list. Verify federal or California certification, AIR, EGR, catalyst diagnostics, rear oxygen sensors, EVAP type, fuel-tank pressure sensing, and any case learn requirements. A matching engine and transmission do not satisfy this gate.
4. **Verify the donor.** Require a stock donor with the same engine, 4L80E, driveline, and emissions configuration. Confirm its OS and segment IDs from the file rather than its filename.
5. **Validate the XDF.** Open an untouched donor BIN with the exact OS-matched XDF. Check several known scalars and tables against an independent definition or commercial tool. Confirm that a no-change save does not alter unrelated bytes.
6. **Validate the image.** Verify segment checksums and compare the final file with the source. Preserve the original image and a recovery path before a full OS write.

## 2002 OS evaluation

`12212156` should not be the default 2002 update candidate for Big Blue. The OS matrix assigns it to 2002 LS1 applications, and the only reviewed 4L80E BIN uses a 5.7L L31 van calibration. A matching XDF only proves that the file can be interpreted with a definition intended for that OS.

Research `12209203` instead because the OS matrix assigns it to 2002 trucks. Keep the update blocked until an original stock 2002 6.0L LQ4 and 4L80E donor is sourced with matching driveline and emissions content. If no exact donor can be verified, remain on the recovered original OS or evaluate the exact 2001 `12202088` donor through the same gates.
